# Product Principles

## 1

Consistency over creativity.

The interface must always behave predictably.

---

## 2

Speed is a feature.

Every common task should require the minimum possible number of interactions.

---

## 3

Never surprise the user.

Critical actions always require confirmation.

---

## 4

The system should always explain what is happening.

Loading.

Errors.

Success.

Empty states.

Everything must communicate.

---

## 5

Data before decoration.

Visual effects must never reduce readability.

---

## 6

Everything should feel immediate.

Optimistic updates whenever possible.

Skeletons before spinners.

---

## 7

Accessibility is mandatory.

Keyboard navigation.

Proper focus.

Screen readers.

Contrast.

---

## 8

Every screen should answer one question:

"What is the user trying to accomplish?"

Anything else is secondary.

---

## 9

The product should teach itself.

Users should rarely need documentation.

---

## 10

Professionalism through simplicity.

No unnecessary complexity.
