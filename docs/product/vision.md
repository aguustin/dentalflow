# Vision

## Mission

DentalFlow exists to simplify the daily operation of dental clinics.

The platform should reduce administrative work, eliminate unnecessary manual processes and allow professionals to focus on patient care.

---

## Vision

Become the operating system for modern dental clinics.

Every daily operation should be manageable from a single platform.

---

## Target Market

Small and medium dental clinics.

Private practices.

Clinics with multiple professionals.

Growing organizations requiring centralized management.

---

## Problems We Solve

Patient management.

Appointment scheduling.

Staff organization.

Billing visibility.

Permission management.

Operational efficiency.

---

## Product Philosophy

The software should disappear.

Users should think about their patients, not about the system.

---

## Success Metrics

Time required to register a patient.

Time required to schedule an appointment.

Average daily active users.

Retention.

Subscription conversion.

Customer satisfaction.

---

## What We Are NOT

We are not an ERP.

We are not accounting software.

We are not an electronic medical record replacement.

We are a clinic operations platform.
