# Design System

## Principles

Minimal.

Professional.

Clean.

Fast.

Accessible.

---

## Visual Language

Rounded corners.

Soft shadows.

Generous spacing.

High contrast.

Limited color palette.

---

## Typography

Clear hierarchy.

Readable sizes.

Comfortable line spacing.

---

## Components

Every component must exist only once.

Reusable.

Composable.

Accessible.

---

## Responsive

Desktop first.

Tablet optimized.

Mobile supported.

---

## Themes

Light.

Dark.

Brand customization (future).

---

## Animations

Fast.

Subtle.

Functional.

Never decorative.

---

## Accessibility

WCAG AA.

Keyboard navigation.

Focus visible.

ARIA support.
